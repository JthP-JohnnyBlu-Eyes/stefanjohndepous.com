About me

I follow the voice beneath the noise

I'm Stefan, a poet and coach from the Netherlands. For most of my life I have been drawn to what lies under the surface: dreams, symbols, old alchemical texts, and the quiet knowing that arrives before the mind can explain it.

When I first read my Gene Keys profile, one line stood out: trust your intuition and everything becomes clear. My life's work sits in Gene Key 57, the Gift of Intuition. My purpose sits in Gene Key 61, the Gift of Inspiration: to move beyond every definition of myself and help others do the same.

So this site is that, in practice. Poems that came from listening. Books that changed me. And sessions where I help you hear your own inner voice clearly enough to follow it.

Sacred geometry

The torus, the shape consciousness takes

A torus is a field that feeds itself. Energy rises through a central axis, flows out over the surface and returns through the same centre, endlessly. You find the pattern in an apple, a smoke ring, the magnetic field of the Earth, and the way the heart's field is often pictured.

I use it as a map for inner life. What we send out returns to us through the centre. A Shadow avoided keeps circling. A Gift lived keeps renewing itself, and in that flow the self becomes both still point and movement.

The still point

The empty centre where everything passes through. In practice: silence, breath, the moment before an intuition speaks.

The outflow

Expression: the poem written, the decision taken, the step away from the status quo that intuition asks for.

The return

Integration. Experience folds back into the centre and becomes wisdom, so the next turn begins from a deeper place.

Depth psychology

Carl Jung and the descent inward

Jung saw the psyche as a living landscape with depths we rarely visit. His map pairs naturally with the Gene Keys: the Shadow he describes is the same raw material the Gene Keys ask us to transform.

Persona

het masker

The face we show the world. Useful, and never the whole of us. A brand built honestly lets the persona carry the soul instead of hiding it.

Shadow

de schaduw

Everything we refuse to see in ourselves. Met with compassion, it gives back the energy it has been holding.

Anima & Animus

de innerlijke ander

The contrasexual image within, guide to the unconscious and source of our deepest projections in love.

Archetypes

Patterns of the collective unconscious: the Hero, the Mother, the Trickster, the Wise Old Man, alive in myths and in our dreams.

The Great Work

Alchemy, the art of inner gold

The old alchemists worked on lead and mercury, yet the text kept speaking of the soul. Jung recognised their stages as the stages of individuation. The Magnum Opus has four colours.

Stage one

Nigredo

the blackening

Dissolution and the dark night. Old forms break down. In Gene Keys terms, meeting the Shadow fully.

Stage two

Albedo

the whitening

Washing and clarity. The soul is purified and reflects light like the moon. The Gift begins to shine.

Stage three

Citrinitas

the yellowing

Dawn of the inner sun. Reflected light becomes one's own light, and wisdom becomes lived.

Stage four

Rubedo

the reddening

The union of opposites, the philosopher's stone. Spirit lives in matter. Here the Siddhi is remembered.

Solve et coagula: dissolve and bind again. The same rhythm as the torus, outward and back to the centre.
